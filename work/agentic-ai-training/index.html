Agentic AI training for non-technical teams

Internal training that translated agentic workflows and prompt engineering into practical applications for each department.

TrainingAgentic workflowsPrompt engineering

Problem

Agentic workflows and prompt engineering are unfamiliar outside engineering. Describing them in engineers' terms does not help a finance or HR team decide what to try on Monday.

Process

I designed and delivered the training myself, translating the concepts into practical, department-level applications for non-technical teams across the company.

Result

Teams outside engineering got a working picture of what agentic tooling can and cannot do, framed around their own work rather than mine. It is the same instinct as the agent system, applied to people: make the boundaries clear, then make the tool usable.

Employer systems, repositories, work items and client names are deliberately left out. The mechanism is the point.
