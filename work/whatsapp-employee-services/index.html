WhatsApp payroll and employee services

Employees request payslips, leave information and IRP5 documents over WhatsApp, backed by Meta's platform and Sage payroll data.

WhatsApp / MetaSageDeploymentsIncident response

Problem

Employees wanted payroll answers where they already are: WhatsApp. The service had to identify the right employee reliably and hand over sensitive documents such as payslips and IRP5s.

Process

Part of a three-person team at Genghis Technology. I took one client deployment through to production myself and then turned it into a repeatable procedure.

Architecture

Meta's WhatsApp platform on one side, Sage payroll data on the other, with a webhook-driven service between them.

Implementation

  • A phone-number variant parser that matches an incoming number against payroll records across the different formats the same number can arrive in.
  • A full end-to-end client deployment: Meta configuration, webhook setup, application configuration and the deployment pipeline.
  • That deployment documented into a repeatable procedure, so the next one is not a rediscovery.

Reliability

I share rotating deployment duty, investigate incidents using Application Insights, and communicate directly with the client through production incidents.

Result

A live employee-services channel and, just as usefully, a documented, repeatable way to stand up the next one.

Employer systems, repositories, work items and client names are deliberately left out. The mechanism is the point.
